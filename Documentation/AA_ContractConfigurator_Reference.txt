# Archer Aerospace – Launch Services Contracts

This contract pack adds commercial launch services provided by Archer Aerospace, allowing players to outsource satellite deployments instead of flying every mission themselves.

These contracts are designed for role-play and realism-leaning career games, similar to NASA contracting commercial launch providers.

---

## WHAT THIS PACK ADDS

Single-Satellite Launch Services

* Delivery of one unmanned satellite to:

  * 0° inclination (equatorial)
  * 45° inclination
  * 90° inclination (polar)
* Fixed circular orbit at 300 km
* 30-day lead time
* Satellite is delivered and transferred to player control on completion

3-Satellite Constellation Launch Services

* Deployment of three identical satellites
* All satellites:

  * Share the same orbit
  * Are evenly spaced 120° apart
* Available at:

  * 0° / 45° / 90° inclination
* 42-day lead time
* All satellites are transferred to the player on completion

---

## COSTS & GAMEPLAY BALANCE

* Contracts use negative funds (you pay Archer Aerospace)
* Costs are based on the sticker cost of an equivalent launch vehicle
* Reusability savings are not passed to the player (realistic commercial pricing)
* Prestige is locked to Trivial to prevent reward scaling

Default pricing:

Single satellite launch

* Accept: 1,700
* Complete: 15,400
* Total: 17,100

3-satellite constellation launch

* Accept: 5,100
* Complete: 46,200
* Total: 51,300

## HOW TO MODIFY THE CONTRACTS

Change the satellite being launched:

Replace the craftURL line in the contract:

craftURL = Archer Areospace/Contracts/VesselTemplates/AS-Test-K.craft

with another .craft file placed in the same folder.

Important:
Craft files should only contain stock modules unless you intentionally require other mods.

---

Change orbit altitude:

Modify the SMA value in the ORBIT block:

SMA = 900000

Kerbin radius is 600,000 m.
Orbit altitude = SMA minus 600,000.

Example:
500 km orbit = SMA = 1100000

---

Change inclination:

Each contract is fixed to one inclination:

INC = 0   (equatorial)
INC = 45  (mid-inclination)
INC = 90  (polar)

---

Change lead time:

Edit the Duration parameter:

duration = 30d

Examples:
14d  – fast commercial cadence
60d  – realistic manifest delay
120d – development or first-article launch

---

Change pricing:

Negative values deduct funds from the player:

advanceFunds = -1700
rewardFunds  = -15400

Advance is paid on acceptance.
Reward is paid on completion.
